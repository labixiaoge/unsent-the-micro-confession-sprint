One micro-confession in exactly two parts: a To: line naming who it was meant for, and the unsent message itself, one line written exactly as it was typed, with no explanation after it.

> To: my ex's mother
>
> I still send your birthday card every year from a different post office, because if he ever found out he'd think I was trying to come back, and I'm not — I just miss you.

The addressee and the message need each other: "your birthday card" and "he" only resolve through "my ex's mother". The detail is specific and honest — a different post office every year, so the postmark never gives the sender away — and the reason it stayed unsent is felt in the words, not stated: afraid of being misread as trying to come back, when the truth is simpler and quieter than that.

## Run it

```sh
node main.ts
```

The program writes exactly those two lines to `/output/entry.txt` (override with `OUTPUT_PATH` for local testing). No trailing newline: 189 characters, 191 bytes.
